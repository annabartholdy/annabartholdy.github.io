About

MLLS is an initiative based in Copenhagen bringing together groups from DTU, the University of Copenhagen and collaborators across industry. We organize community events, talks, and reading groups focused on practical and theoretical aspects of ML for biology, medicine, and the natural sciences.

Want to present your work or propose a meetup? Drop us a line below — we’d love to hear from you.

What we do

  • Seminars & invited talks
  • Reading groups & paper clubs
  • Hands-on workshops
  • Collaborative projects
